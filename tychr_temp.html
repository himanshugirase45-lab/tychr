How It Works

A smooth and easy transition

01

Tell Us What You Need


 Share your academic goals, challenges, and future aspirations with us. Whether it’s excelling in IB subjects, preparing for board exams, or mapping out your dream university, we take the time to listen and understand.
 This first step ensures that your journey is built around YOU, not a one-size-fits-all approach.

02

Personalized Tutor Match

We carefully recommend a tutor who not only has subject expertise but also aligns with your learning style and personality. Our counselors handpick mentors who can bring out your best.
 Think of it as finding your academic “match”—someone who makes learning easier, motivating, and impactful.

 

03

Experience a Trial Session

 Step into a trial class to see your tutor in action. This interactive session allows you to get comfortable, ask questions, and experience how TYCHR turns complex concepts into clarity.
 No commitment—just a chance to feel the difference before you decide.

04

Feedback & Perfect Fit

After your trial, share your honest feedback. Loved the class?? Fantastic—we confirm your tutor and create a tailored plan. Need adjustments? We’ll find another match until you’re 100% confident.
 Because your satisfaction comes first, and the right mentor makes all the difference.

05

Begin Your Learning Journey

Once your tutor is finalized, your personalized academic roadmap begins. From regular sessions and progress tracking to continuous support, TYCHR ensures you stay on track toward your goals.
 This isn’t just tutoring—it’s a partnership in your success, from the first session to the day you achieve your dreams.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes our platform different from other learning solutions?

Our platform is designed specifically for achieving higher grades in global curricula. We combine expert tutors, personalized study plans, real exam strategies, and continuous progress tracking to help students improve scores efficiently and confidently.

How do I get started with my learning journey?

Our platform is designed for success across a majority of international curricula. You can get started by booking a free demo session. During the demo, we assess your current level, understand your target score, and recommend a customized study plan tailored to your goals.

What kind of support do you provide to learners?

We provide complete support like digital test format practice, time-management strategies, section-wise preparation, and real exam–style mock tests to help students feel fully confident on test day.

Can I access the content on mobile devices?

Yes. Our platform is fully responsive and accessible on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices, allowing students to learn anytime, anywhere.

What are the pricing options available?

We offer flexible pricing plans based on the level of support, duration, and learning requirements. Our team will help you choose the most suitable plan during the free demo session.

How do you ensure the quality of your educational content?

 All content is created and reviewed by experienced IB/AP educators. Practice questions and study materials are regularly updated to align with the latest exam patterns and difficulty levels.
